# Jotty on TOS privacy policy

The NAS administrator operates this installation. Jotty processes account information, sessions, API keys, notes, checklists and sharing metadata to provide its services. The package stores data and configuration on the NAS. Logs can include addresses and operation details. Shared links can make selected content available to people authorized by the link settings.

Updates contact package and container registries. Optional identity providers, external integrations or content referenced by a note can receive requests when enabled or opened. Administrators choose those services and review their destinations. The package sets the framework telemetry opt-out.

Content, settings and logs remain until edited, deleted or rotated by the administrator or an authorized account. Backups and exports can retain older copies. Contact the NAS administrator for access, correction or deletion. Protect data and configuration backups, authentication material and shared links.

Use the repository for package support and keep private content and credentials out of public issue reports.
